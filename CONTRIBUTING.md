# Contributing to WireFrame

WireFrame is source-available, not open source. The Owner (Ed13S) decides what goes in.

Have an improvement or a fix? You are welcome to suggest it:

1. Open an **issue** describing the idea, or
2. Open a **pull request** with the change (see the LICENCE, section 3).

The Owner will review it and merge it if he likes it. Please keep each pull request small
and focused, and say how you tested it. Nothing is merged automatically.
